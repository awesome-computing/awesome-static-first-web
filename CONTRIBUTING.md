# Contributing

Thanks for considering a contribution to Awesome Static-First Web!

## Adding a resource

- It must be real, actively maintained (or a stable, still-relevant historical reference), and genuinely relevant to the static-first vs server-rendering decision.
- Add it to the most relevant existing category, or propose a new category if it doesn't fit anywhere.
- One-line description: what it is and why it belongs here, no marketing copy.
- Keep the list alphabetical within a category where practical.

## Adding evidence or a case study

Qualitative write-ups, benchmarks, or real "we moved from SSR to static (or vice versa) and here's what happened" case studies are especially valuable — open a PR or an issue with a link and a short summary of what it shows.

## Reporting a stale or broken link

Open an issue, or just send a PR removing/fixing it.

## Style

- Keep entries factual. No unverified performance claims or invented statistics.
- Keep the "When to Opt for Static vs Server Rendering" and "When You Actually Need SSR" sections honest — this is a decision guide, not a static-only manifesto.
